How these numbers are calculated
Incidents
Issues reported in the period. Reports marked as duplicates are not counted.
First response
Time from the server receiving the report to the first staff action (acknowledge, comment or change). Elapsed time, not business hours.
Restoration
From the impact start (the reported start time, or the report time) to the instrument being restored (end of its downtime, or resolution). Independent of when the ticket is closed.
Unavailable time and availability
Periods staff marked the instrument unavailable, merged so overlapping issues are counted once, clipped to the period. Availability = 1 - unavailable hours / calendar hours.
Booked hours
EMhub bookings on the instrument in the period (slots, downtime and maintenance excluded). Bookings are not the same as operating hours.
Imported history
Entries imported from older logbooks count as incidents but are excluded from response and restoration times, because their timestamps are not reliable.
